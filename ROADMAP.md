# Roadmap

This is where Mnemo is going next.

The roadmap isn't a promise or a fixed schedule. Plans can change as Mnemo develops, but the principles below are meant to stay the same.

- **Local first.** Your notes, flashcards, mind maps, and study progress are stored on your own computer, in a folder you can open, copy, back up, or delete. You don't need an account to use Mnemo locally.
- **Online features are opt-in.** By default, Mnemo only goes online to check for updates. You can turn update checks off in Settings.
- **No tracking or ads.** Core features are free. Paid services exist to fund the project, and where practical, you'll be able to host them yourself.
- **Your data isn't locked in.** You can export to Markdown, PDF, Anki, CSV, PNG, SVG, and `.mnemo` packages. Newer versions of Mnemo can always open libraries made by older versions. If a library is too new for an older version, Mnemo tells you instead of trying to guess.

## Next: sync and mobile

The next big goal is using Mnemo across multiple devices. Sync and a mobile app are being planned together and will probably arrive in 0.9.

**Sync.** Sync will be optional. Turn it on and Mnemo keeps your library in sync across your devices. Leave it off and Mnemo continues to work as it does today.

There will be two ways to sync:

- **Hosted by us.** The easiest option if you don't want to run a server. The price will cover the cost of the service and help fund ongoing development. We'll keep it affordable and competitive with similar apps. There may also be a free tier.
- **Self-hosted.** Run the sync server yourself and get the same sync features.

We haven't decided whether devices will connect through an account, by linking directly, or whether both options will be available. You will only need an account to use our hosted service.

**Mobile app.** Use your notes, flashcards, and mind maps on your phone and keep them in sync with your computer.

**More note blocks.** Notes are still missing some blocks available in other apps. First up are collapsible blocks and layouts with up to four columns.

## Later

These are planned, but don't have a target release yet.

- **Audio on flashcards.** Record or attach audio to a card.
- **More ways to practise.** Study games and other practice modes alongside reviews and tests.
- **Extensions.** Install modules and themes made by others, or make your own. This will also include importing custom themes.
- **Downloads inside the app.** Get themes, language packs, spell-check dictionaries, and study material from inside Mnemo instead of waiting for an app update.

## Not planned

- Requiring an account to use Mnemo on your own computer
- Analytics, telemetry, or ads
- Putting core features behind a paywall
